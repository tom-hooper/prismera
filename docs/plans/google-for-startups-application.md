# Google for Startups Cloud Program Application

Draft prepared 2026-08-06 for Prismera Pty Ltd (Prismera MSP, Melbourne).
Angle: sovereign data-residence platform that secures customer services end to end, with
domestic (Australian) data storage and self-managed service design.

## Elevator pitch

Prismera is Australia's sovereign data-residence platform for managed IT security. We
secure a customer's entire IT environment end to end, from endpoints to cloud workloads,
while guaranteeing their data never leaves Australia. Every customer environment runs on
our self-managed platform hosted in Australian data centres, monitored 24/7 by our own
security operations team, so Australian businesses get enterprise-grade protection with
domestic data storage and full operational control.

## What are you building?

Prismera is a data-sovereign security platform delivered as a subscription product.
Customers connect their workstations, servers, and cloud workloads to our platform, which
provides continuous endpoint monitoring, threat detection and response, automated patch
management, and incident investigation and recovery, all from our self-managed security
stack running in Australian regions.

The architecture is built around sovereignty by design: data at rest remains in Australia,
all monitoring and management is operated by our own Australian team with no third-party
or offshore handling, and every customer environment is isolated and independently
managed rather than sitting in shared multi-tenant infrastructure. Prevention, detection,
response, and recovery are delivered end to end under one platform, with full visibility
handed to the customer.

## What problem does it solve?

Australian businesses face two poor options: offshore hyperscale tools that route their
data and support outside the country, or building their own security capability with no
dedicated expertise. Regulatory pressure is intensifying, with Privacy Act reforms, APRA
CPS 234, and the Essential Eight all demanding stronger security and clearer data
handling. Customers increasingly ask two questions: where does our data live, and who
touches it? Prismera answers both with a platform that guarantees Australian data
residence and end-to-end protection as a single self-managed service.

## Market and business model

We target Australian SMBs and regulated professions, including legal, accounting,
financial services, and health practices with 10 to 500 endpoints, plus the MSP channel
for white-label deployment. Revenue is a monthly per-endpoint subscription with tiered
plans. The platform model means predictable recurring revenue and product-led growth.

## How will you use Google Cloud?

We run our platform on Google Cloud in Australian regions, including australia-southeast1
and australia-southeast2, so customer data at rest never leaves Australia. Google Cloud
hosts our platform management plane, security telemetry and log storage, and analytics
for threat detection. We plan to build AI-assisted detection and response capabilities on
Vertex AI and Gemini to strengthen our end-to-end protection and move toward the AI-first
credit tier.

## Funding stage

Bootstrapped to date. We are applying for the Start tier and plan to seek seed funding
within the next 12 months once the platform has validated customer traction.

## What the credits will fund

Credits will cover platform infrastructure in Australian regions, security telemetry
storage, and R&D on our AI-assisted detection engine, extending our runway while we
validate the model with early customers.

## Standing rules for the application (do not break)

1. Credits cover Prismera's own platform spend. Customers subscribe to the platform;
   Prismera pays Google Cloud. Never structure it as billing client infrastructure
   through to Google Cloud, that reads as reselling.
2. "Sovereign" wording is defensible: "data at rest never leaves Australia" and
   "operated by our team in Australia". Do not claim no data ever crosses borders.
3. Present as a product company. Never describe Prismera as a consultancy, agency, or
   dev shop in the application or on the site. No per-hour billing language.
