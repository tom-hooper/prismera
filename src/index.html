Frequently Asked Questions

What does a managed service provider (MSP) actually do?

An MSP takes ownership of your technology: proactive monitoring, maintenance, patching, helpdesk support, backups and security, for a predictable monthly fee. You get enterprise-grade IT without hiring an in-house team. See our services for the full picture.

Is my data really kept in Australia?

Yes. Prismera is a sovereign data-residence platform: your data at rest never leaves Australia, it is stored in Australian data centres, and no offshore team handles it. Everything is operated by our Melbourne-based team. Details are on the platform page.

What is the Essential Eight and why does it matter?

The Essential Eight is the Australian Signals Directorate's set of eight mitigation strategies for cyber security. It is the practical baseline Australian businesses use to reduce risk and limit the impact of attacks. We implement and manage it for you, including maturity assessments and compliance roadmaps.

How much do managed IT services cost in Australia?

Plans start at $20 per user per month and scale with your business. Every plan includes onboarding, migration support and access to our Australian help desk, with no lock-in contracts. See our pricing or book a free health check for a tailored quote.

Do you lock clients into long-term contracts?

No. We believe in flexible, month-to-month agreements that reflect our confidence in the service we deliver. We earn your business every month.

How do I get started with Prismera?

Book a free IT health check or contact us. We will review your environment, show you exactly where you stand, and put together a plan that fits your business and your budget.
