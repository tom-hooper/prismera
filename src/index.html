Frequently Asked Questions

What does a managed service provider actually do?

We take ownership of your technology: monitoring, maintenance, patching, helpdesk, backups and security, for a predictable monthly fee. You get the kind of IT cover a big company has, without hiring an in-house team. See our services for the full picture.

How much do managed IT services cost?

Plans start at $99 per user per month and scale with your business. Every plan includes onboarding, migration support and access to our Australian help desk, with no lock-in contracts. See our pricing or book a free health check for a tailored quote.

Is my data really kept in Australia?

Yes. Your data at rest never leaves Australia, it is stored in Australian data centres, and no offshore team handles it. Everything is operated by our Melbourne-based team. Details are on the platform page.

Do you lock clients into long-term contracts?

No. We work month to month and we earn your business every month. You can read exactly what we commit to in writing before you sign anything.
