Rate Schedule

Our rates, charges and the conditions that apply to them

Version 1.0. Effective 6 October 2026. Amounts are in Australian dollars and exclude GST unless stated otherwise.

This is the Rate Schedule referred to in our General Terms and Conditions and in your Agreement. It sets out our rates, the charges that go with them, and the conditions that apply.

If a rate appears in both this schedule and your Proposal or Agreement, the rate in your Proposal or Agreement applies. Your Proposal is the authoritative price for your engagement.

1. Managed services plans

Plans are priced per user, per month. A user is a person we support, whether they have a company computer or only a managed account.

Per user, per month, excluding GST. One-year term.
PlanSeatsRate
Starter10 to 19$99

Minimum user counts apply to each plan: 10 on Starter, 20 on Business, and 40 on Enterprise. Enterprise is quoted for your environment, and the rate shown is the starting point for that conversation.

Every plan includes onboarding and migration support, access to our Melbourne help desk during business hours, and continuous monitoring and alerting. The plan cards on our pricing page list what each plan includes.

2. Terms, and how the monthly rate comes down

One year is the minimum term, and the one-year rate is the highest monthly rate we charge. There is no month-to-month or shorter-term rate.

Committing for longer fixes a lower monthly rate for the whole term. Each rate below is a percentage below the one-year rate for the same plan.

Term discount, applied to the one-year rate.

1 year (minimum term)Standard rate

A rate fixed for a term stays fixed for that term, other than the annual increase in section 3. The term ladder appears on your Proposal with your own numbers on it.

3. Annual increase

Where your Agreement runs for more than one year, your monthly fee increases by 3% on each anniversary after the first year. This is stated in your Agreement, so it arrives as a known part of the term and not as a surprise.

4. Minimum engagement and seat changes

The minimum monthly engagement is $990 excluding GST. Below that we will re-scope the work, or quote it as work outside an Agreement at the rates in section 5.

Because plans are priced per user, your monthly fee moves with your headcount. Tell us about starters and departures and we adjust the next invoice.

5. Work outside your plan: hourly rates

Work that is not covered by your plan is Out of Scope Work, and is charged at these rates. The same rates apply to managed clients and to one-off work.

Business Hours means 8:00am to 6:00pm on a Business Day, and a Business Day is a day that is not a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Victoria.

Remote work has a 30 minute minimum and is then charged in 15 minute increments. On-site work has a one hour minimum and is then charged in 30 minute increments. There is no separate trip or travel charge, because travel is part of the on-site rate.

Emergency Upgrade. If you ask us to work a Service Request above the priority it would otherwise be classified as, or you lodge it through one of the escalation contacts in your Agreement, it is charged at the emergency rate of double the applicable hourly rate.

On-site work beyond the allowance included in your Agreement is charged at the on-site hourly rate. Project and one-time work, such as a migration, an equipment install or an office move, is quoted to you upfront from these rates before the work starts.

6. Third-party products

Licences and subscriptions we procure for you are charged at cost plus 10%, shown as a single line covering procurement, licence management and vendor liaison.

Hardware and equipment is quoted separately for each engagement, before you commit to it.

Where your Agreement sets a different arrangement for a particular product, your Agreement applies.

7. Onboarding

Onboarding and migration work is quoted for your environment as part of your Proposal, and it is separate from your monthly fee. Where we discount that fee, the discount is stated against the full fee, and the amount discounted may become payable if your Agreement ends early, as set out in your Agreement.

8. Invoicing and payment

8.1 Monthly fees are invoiced in advance and are payable within 14 days of the invoice date, unless your Agreement says otherwise.

8.2 Amounts are in Australian dollars and exclude GST. GST is added where it applies.

8.3 You can pay however you like. Our rates carry the cost of payment processing, so there is no card surcharge and no discount for paying another way.

8.4 If an invoice is not paid, we may suspend the services once it is 30 or more days overdue and after we have given you 10 Business Days' written warning. Fees continue to accrue while services are suspended, and we may charge interest on overdue amounts and our reasonable costs of recovery.

8.5 If you dispute an invoice, tell us in writing within 14 days. Pay the part you do not dispute, and we will work through the rest with you.

9. Changes to this schedule

We may vary this schedule from time to time as our costs, our services and our Vendors change. The version that applies to you is the one published on the date you accept our Proposal. Where a change materially and adversely affects you, we give you at least 30 days' written notice before it takes effect, and you may end your Agreement in accordance with its terms.
