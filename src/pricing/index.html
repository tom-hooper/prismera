Pricing Questions

How does per-user pricing work?

You pay a fixed rate per user per month, and that rate covers the person's managed devices, email, security stack and support. There are no separate line items for devices, services or add-ons, so the monthly cost is predictable and easy to budget for.

What is the minimum number of users?

Starter plans start at 5 users and Business plans at 10 users. Smaller teams are welcome too, contact us for a tailored quote that fits.

What is included in the per-user rate?

Every plan includes onboarding, migration support and access to our Australian help desk. Each tier adds more, from the full security stack and backup and recovery on Business, through to dedicated staffing and priority SLAs on Enterprise. See the plan cards above for the full breakdown.

Are there setup fees or lock-in contracts?

No setup fees and no lock-in contracts. Pricing is month to month, and we will always tell you what you are paying for and why.

What happens as my team grows?

Your cost grows by one predictable per-user rate per person. When you outgrow your current tier, we will help you move up smoothly, no renegotiation surprises.

How do I get an exact quote?

Book a free IT health check and we will assess your environment, size the right plan, and give you a tailored quote with no obligation. See the health check page to get started.

Not sure which plan fits?

Book a free IT health check and we will show you where your business stands, then recommend the right plan.
