The Prismera Platform

Sovereign data residence, end-to-end security, self-managed by design

Your data stays in Australia. Your security never sleeps.

The Prismera Platform is a sovereign data-residence platform that secures your entire IT environment end to end. Customer data at rest stays in Australia, hosted in Australian data centres, and every layer of the platform is operated by our own Melbourne-based team. No offshore handling, no shared tenancy, no ambiguity about where your data lives or who touches it.
