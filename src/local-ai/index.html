Why keep it in the office

Your data stays where it already lives

Most AI tools work by sending your question, and often your document, to a provider overseas. For a clinic, a law practice or anyone holding client files, that is the part that needs a second look.

This machine keeps the model on site. Staff ask questions in the browser, the model reads your documents on your own network, and no prompt or file leaves the building.

The machine

One box, about the size of a shoebox

It uses AMD's Ryzen AI Max+ 395, a processor with the graphics built in and 128GB of memory shared between the two. That shared memory is what lets a machine this small hold a large AI model.

Where it stops

What this box will not do

We would rather tell you now than have you find out in month two.

  • infoIt is not ChatGPT. A 70B open model drafts, summarises and answers from your documents well. It is weaker on obscure general knowledge and on arithmetic with many steps.
  • infoIt does not run your file server, your practice software or your email. We keep the machine to AI work.
  • infoIt needs looking after. Someone on your side keeps the document folder current, and we refresh the model twice a year.
  • infoIf the box is off, the assistant is off. There is no cloud fallback in the pilot.

Questions

What people ask us

How is this different from Copilot or ChatGPT?

Copilot works inside Microsoft 365 and sends your prompts to Microsoft. This runs an open model on a machine in your office. It suits businesses whose files sit on a server or in shared folders, and anyone who cannot send client information to an overseas provider.

What does it cost to run each month?

Electricity, roughly what a desktop PC uses. There is no per-user AI subscription. Support after the first twelve months is quoted at renewal, and you can decline it and keep using the machine.

Can staff use it from home?

Yes, over your existing VPN. If you would rather not open that up, we can host the same model in your cloud tenancy instead.

What happens to our documents at the end?

The index and the copies on the machine are wiped in front of you, or the drive is removed and handed over, whichever you prefer. Nothing is retained by us.

Why does it need 128GB of memory?

The memory is shared between the processor and the graphics. A 70B model at 4-bit needs around 40GB just for the weights, plus room for the document index and the conversation. 128GB is the size that runs those models comfortably on one small machine.

Do we need to change our internet or network?

No. Updates and support use your normal connection. We ask for one network port and a power point.

Is this a Prismera managed service?

It can be. The pilot is a one-off purchase with twelve months of support. Clients on a managed agreement can add it to their plan instead.
