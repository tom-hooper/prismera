Where we work

Prismera is Melbourne based, and most of our clients sit inside 20 km of the CBD: professional services firms, clinics and practices, trades businesses with an office, and not-for-profits that have outgrown break-fix support. They are mostly 10 to 40 staff, which is big enough to need real IT and small enough that a person still answers the phone.

Support arrives two ways. Remote covers the daily work: accounts, email, devices, monitoring, patching, and the questions that take ten minutes. On-site covers the jobs that need hands in the room: a new starter’s laptop, a failed switch, a site survey, a network cutover, or a team that wants to talk to someone face to face.
